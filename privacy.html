Privacy Policy

Sudais Studio Uploader · Last updated 25 October 2026

Sudais Studio Uploader is a private tool operated by Sudais (contact: lajpalbari89@gmail.com). It uploads videos created by its owner to YouTube channels that the owner controls. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses. With the channel owner's permission, granted through Google's sign-in screen, the tool uses YouTube API Services to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, playlists, visibility and publish times, and read the upload status of those videos.

What it stores. The access tokens Google issues for each channel, and the details entered for each upload, stored on the owner's private home device. It does not collect, store or share personal data about viewers or any other people.

Sharing. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to Google, as required to upload the videos.

Google's policies. This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy applies to data Google processes.

Removing access. The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at Google security settings, after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the device within 30 days.
