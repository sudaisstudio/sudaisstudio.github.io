Privacy Policy

Sudais Studio Uploader · Last updated 27 October 2026

Sudais Studio Uploader is a private tool operated by Sudais (contact: lajpalbari89@gmail.com). It publishes videos created by its owner to accounts the owner controls. It is not offered to the public.

Which services it connects to. With the account owner’s permission, granted through each platform’s own sign-in screen, the tool uses YouTube API Services, the Meta Graph API (Facebook Pages and Instagram) and the TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API.

What it accesses. Only what is needed to publish and review the owner’s own content: basic account information (such as the account name and avatar shown during posting), the ability to upload and publish videos with their title or caption, cover, privacy setting and publish time, and statistics of the owner’s own videos and accounts (such as views, likes and followers).

What it stores. The access tokens each platform issues, and the details entered for each upload, stored only on the owner’s private home device. It does not collect, store or share personal data about viewers, followers or any other people.

Sharing. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform being posted to, as required to publish the video.

Platform policies. By using YouTube features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; the Google Privacy Policy applies to data Google processes. Meta’s Privacy Policy and TikTok’s Privacy Policy apply to data those platforms process.

Retention and deletion. Tokens are kept only while the account stays connected. The owner can revoke access at any time: for Google at Google account permissions, for Facebook and Instagram in the Facebook settings under Apps and websites, and for TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. After access is revoked, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted from the device within 30 days. To request deletion of any data, email lajpalbari89@gmail.com.

Contact. lajpalbari89@gmail.com
